## 💎 Visual Art Direction & Identity

- **Inspiration:** Aurem Creative Agency Theme (editorial layout, dark luxury aesthetic, intentional asymmetry, purposeful negative space, refined typography).
- **Primary Background:** `#0b0f19` (Deep Obsidian Navy)
- **Secondary Background:** `#111827` (Charcoal Slate)
- **Card Background:** `#151d2d` (Midnight Glass Card)
- **Electric Gradient Blue:** `linear-gradient(135deg, #00f2fe 0%, #4facfe 50%, #0061ff 100%)`
- **Brand Logo:** `PKS` with animated electric cyan dot
- **Typography:** Google Fonts `Poppins` (Weights: 300, 400, 500, 600, 700, 800) with fluid `clamp()` sizing.
- **Micro-Interactions:** Glassmorphic sticky navbar, dual counter-rotating orbit rings, typewriter animation, interactive magnetic buttons, 3D card tilts, and instant-play video library.
