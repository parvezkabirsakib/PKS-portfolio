Personal Identity

MD Parvez Kabir Sakib

CSE Student, Creative Developer & Content Creator

I am currently pursuing my degree in Computer Science and Engineering (CSE) at Daffodil International University in Dhaka, Bangladesh. My academic journey provides a solid foundation in computational thinking, data structures, and algorithmic principles, which I actively translate into intuitive, responsive front-end engineering.

My true passion lies at the intersection of aesthetic design and clean code. In frontend development, I focus on crafting lightweight, accessible, and high-performance interfaces using modern HTML5, CSS3, and ES6+ JavaScript. I believe that thoughtful spacing, deliberate typography, and subtle micro-interactions turn good websites into unforgettable digital experiences.

As a WordPress specialist, I leverage theme customization, dynamic CMS architecture, and bespoke styling to create functional web solutions for businesses and personal portfolios. In parallel, my enthusiasm for content creation and video production drives me to communicate tech topics visually through Adobe Premiere Pro and Adobe Audition on YouTube.

I am continuously learning, exploring modern web paradigms, and always eager to build meaningful, visually engaging projects that leave an impression.
